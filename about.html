ABOUT AEROTECH PROJECTS

Curiosity that takes flight.

A student-led aerospace engineering portfolio focused on learning through design, analysis and research.

Our purpose

AeroTech Projects presents aerospace engineering concepts and project work in an organized format for students, educators, researchers and aerospace enthusiasts.

Our mission

To connect engineering fundamentals with practical design challenges, document the learning process and encourage thoughtful exploration of aerospace technologies.

Areas of interest

  • Aircraft preliminary design and aerodynamics
  • Rocket propulsion and thermal management
  • Planetary rovers and space exploration systems
  • CAD modelling, structural analysis and simulation

Tools and methods

Projects may use SolidWorks, Fusion 360, ANSYS, MATLAB, XFLR5 and OpenVSP. Results should be described as completed only when verified.
