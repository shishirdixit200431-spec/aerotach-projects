Projects & technical exploration

Design studies and research themes across aircraft, planetary systems and propulsion.

01 · AIRCRAFT DESIGN

Preliminary Design of a Narrow-Body Passenger Aircraft

A conceptual study for a 120–180 passenger narrow-body aircraft, targeting approximately 5,500 km range and cruise near Mach 0.8 at 35,000–41,000 ft.

OpenVSPXFLR5SolidWorksMATLAB

Focus areas: initial sizing, aerodynamic estimates, wing and tail layout, fuselage arrangement and landing-gear concepts. Values are preliminary assumptions and should be checked against the final project report.

02 · SPACE SYSTEMS

Planetary Rover Chassis

A rover chassis concept inspired by planetary exploration vehicles, modelled using CAD and assessed through structural and thermal simulation workflows.

Aluminium 6061-T6SolidWorksFusion 360ANSYS

Focus areas: chassis geometry, load paths, mass-conscious design and evaluation under defined operating assumptions.

03 · ROCKET PROPULSION

Thermal and Flow Optimization of a Regeneratively Cooled Rocket Nozzle Throat

A research study of cooling-channel structures near a rocket nozzle throat, focusing on flow distribution, heat transfer and thermal management.

CFDHeat TransferCooling ChannelsCHT

Focus areas: literature review, channel geometry comparisons, pressure-drop considerations and conjugate heat-transfer methods where applicable. Work in progress is identified as research in progress.
